# Crop Spread
A Minecraft Fabric mod that makes it easier to plant crops in a large field.

Just plant seeds normally, and the connected farmland blocks will be seeded, as long as there are seeds in your hand.

![Planting demonstration](./cropspread-planting.gif)

Works with Wheat, Beetroots, Carrots and Potatoes. Melon seeds and Pumpkin seeds were deliberately not included, as they usually are not planted side by side.
